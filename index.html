ChannelOS

Short documentary videos, from research to publishing

ChannelOS is a private desktop tool that its owner uses to research real stories, write and narrate short documentary videos, and publish them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public, and only accounts that belong to the owner are connected to it.

How it works with TikTok

  1. The owner connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and authorizes ChannelOS.
  2. For a finished video, the owner chooses one of two options on the publishing screen:
    • Send as a draft: the video is uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox, where they review it and post it from the TikTok app.
    • Post directly: the screen shows the connected account, the caption, who can view the video (no default), whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, the commercial content disclosure, and TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. The video is posted only after the owner confirms.
  3. The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time in ChannelOS.
